Identity (DID)
Registered in the public PLC directory. The account can move to another PDS later, and rotation keys can recover it.
The identity is the handle's web address (did:web: plus the handle), and this server serves its DID document. Before choosing it:
  • The DID is tied to the handle's hostname forever: the handle can change later, the DID can't.
  • There are no rotation keys: whoever controls that hostname controls the identity.
  • Moving to another PDS needs the new PDS, or the owner, to serve the DID document at that hostname.
